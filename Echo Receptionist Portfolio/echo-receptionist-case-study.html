Why it was built

Market friction, then a product response.

Friction

  • After-hours coverage was a service cost, not a desk.
  • Generic IVR could not book a live calendar slot.
  • CRM notes arrived late or not at all.
  • Urgent callers needed a human transfer, not a form.

Response

  • Always-on inbound voice with industry playbooks.
  • Live Google Calendar offers — no double-booking.
  • End-of-call write-back to GoHighLevel.
  • Warm transfer when the playbook flags urgency.

AI layer

What the AI does — and how it actually runs.

Echo is a voice agent with light post-call automation. Claims below match the shipped product: live telephony when keys are present, and a full dashboard simulation when they are not.

Answer

Live voice

Conversation runs through Vapi when keys are configured. Echo uses the client greeting and knowledge — it does not invent coverage or prices.

Qualify

Transcript + score

End-of-call reports supply transcript, summary, and flags. The workspace stores sentiment, stage, and a score.

Book

Calendar slots

Google Calendar is read for open inventory. Echo only offers slots that are free.

Automate

SMS · CRM · callback

Twilio texts confirmations. GoHighLevel gets notes. Outbound uses Vapi + Twilio, or queues in demo mode.

Full picture

What was delivered to the client.

What

A production AI voice receptionist platform: public site, auth, Mission Control, call log, lead pipeline, agent builder, integrations, and Vapi configuration.

Who

Local operators in home services, dental, medspa, legal, and real estate — desks that cannot miss the first call.

Stack

Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind, Framer Motion, Three.js. HMAC session auth. File-backed stores. Optional Vapi, Twilio, GoHighLevel, Google Calendar.

Client deliverable

A live workspace the operator can log into: configure the agent, connect carriers, watch calls, move leads, and run industry playbooks.

Core modules: Mission Control · Call Logs · Lead Pipeline · AI Agent Builder · Integrations · Vapi Configuration.
